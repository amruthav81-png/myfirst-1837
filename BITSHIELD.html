01 / INBOX

Spam email

Bulk messages imitate exchanges, wallet providers, and retailers to steal logins or direct people to lookalike pages.

Check: sender domain, unexpected attachments, login links, and pressure to “verify” an account.

Image: Strizivojna · CC BY-SA 4.0
02 / FEED

Social posts & messages

“Free Bitcoin” giveaways and investment pitches collect contact details, then move targets into private chats or external sites.

Check: off-platform redirects, fake endorsements, and requests for contact info or wallet access.

Image: Wikimedia Commons · Public domain
03 / DISPLAY

Pop-ups & banner ads

Malicious ads can imitate transfer notifications or show a believable fake balance to lure people into a fraudulent claim page.

Check: unexpected balances, countdowns, download prompts, and payment demands to “release” funds.

Image: Packer1028 · CC0
04 / MOBILE

SMS & text messages

Mass texts use urgent calls to action, short links, or “reply to opt in” language to release a fake balance.

Check: unknown sender, reply prompts, shortened links, and requests to confirm or unlock funds.

Image: Wikimedia Commons · Public domain
